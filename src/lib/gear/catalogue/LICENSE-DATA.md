# Gear catalogue data licence

`telescopes.json` and `eyepieces.json` in this directory were entered **by hand** from manufacturer specification
pages and, where a manufacturer page did not show a figure, a reputable retailer's page. Each entry links the page its
numbers were read from in its `source` field. Nothing was extracted in bulk from any database (Stellarium's Oculars
data, AstroBin, astronomy.tools and similar were not used), and no descriptions, images or other text were copied: an
entry holds only facts (aperture, focal length, apparent field of view), a name and aliases.

The two files are released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), independently of the
licence that applies to the application code in this repository.

Brand and product names are trademarks of their respective owners. They are used only to identify the products, and no
affiliation or endorsement is implied.

Apparent fields of view the manufacturer does not publish are estimates, flagged `afovEstimated`. The value used per
eyepiece design is listed in `README.md`, together with how a zoom eyepiece's click stops are derived.
