## Notes

**Brief (owner, 2026-10-17).** Design a new app icon. First step: three proposals as SVG, (1) star-like, (2) ringed planet, (3) free; the owner picks one before anything is replaced. Nothing in `public/`, `pwa-assets.config.mjs`, the manifest or `Layout.astro` changes in this step. Icons, not illustrations: few shapes, a strong silhouette, legible at 16 and 32 px (favicon) and as PWA icons (192, 512, and a maskable variant with its content inside the central 80 %). Colours are Nightfall tokens (`src/styles/global.css`, dark theme block) written as literal hex, since a favicon cannot read CSS variables.

**The proposals** (`evidence/icon-proposals/<n>/`, each with `icon.svg` (full, 512 viewBox, full-bleed square), `favicon.svg` (32 viewBox, rounded tile, simplified) and `maskable.svg` (full-bleed background, marks scaled to 0.8 about the centre, inside the safe circle r = 204.8), plus PNG previews at 16, 32, 180, 192, 512 and maskable 512):

| #   | Folder     | Idea                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Colours (token, dark theme)                                                                                                                                                  |
| --- | ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | `1-star`   | The Topbar's four-point star, redrawn bolder and taller (diffraction-spike proportions) with a small companion star; the favicon drops the companion and fattens the star's waist so it does not read as a plus sign at 16 px. | sky `--zenith` #050916 → `--horizon` #1b2a55; star `--primary` #f2f4f8; companion `--primary-strong` #c3d0ff                                                                 |
| 2   | `2-planet` | A ringed planet, the ring tilted −22° and cut into the planet by a mask so it reads in front of it; a small four-point star top left (not in the favicon, where the planet is larger and the ring thicker).                    | sky `--zenith` → `--horizon`; planet `--primary-strong` #c3d0ff; ring `--marginal` #f1cf6b; star `--primary` #f2f4f8                                                         |
| 3   | `3-free`   | "Horizon star": Tonight's band as a glyph, a warm star over a dusk-glow horizon and a dark ground silhouette (the live sky's silhouette is the app's signature).                                                               | sky `--zenith` → `--horizon` → `--dusk-glow-horizon` #6a3226; ground `--background` #070a12; star #fde68a (the base colour of `--star-accent`, which the token uses at 30 %) |

**Evidence.** `evidence/icon-proposals/comparison.png` (all three at every size on the dark, light and red grounds; the red view runs the icons through the app's `#red-only` luminance filter from `Layout.astro`, as red mode does to every `<img>`; maskable shown in a circle and a squircle crop), `design-specimen.png` (the new "App icon proposals" band on `/design` in dark, light and red). Regenerate with `render.mjs` and `capture-design.mjs` (dev server on port 4413) in that folder.

**/design.** The band reads the SVGs with an `import.meta.glob` over `/context/*/app-icon/evidence/icon-proposals/*/*.svg` (`?raw`, eager), so it keeps working after archiving and renders dashed placeholders if the files go; the strings are server-only and dead-code-eliminated in production (the page 404s outside dev), checked: no proposal reaches `dist/client` or `dist/server`. Once an icon is picked and moved to `public/`, the band should read it from there (or go).

**Current setup to replace later.** `public/pwa-icon.svg` (24 viewBox, the Topbar star in `--primary` on `--zenith`, full-bleed) is the single source; `npm run icons:build` (`pwa-assets-generator`, `pwa-assets.config.mjs`) writes the committed PNGs `pwa-64x64.png`, `pwa-192x192.png`, `pwa-512x512.png`, `maskable-icon-512x512.png` (padding 0.15 on `#050916`) and `apple-touch-icon-180x180.png`. The browser-tab favicon is not part of that: `favicons: []`, and `public/favicon.png` (32×32) is still the starter's image from the bootstrap commit (9d47743), not the star, so the tab has never shown the Sidereus mark. `Layout.astro` links `/favicon.png` (PNG only, no SVG favicon, no `/favicon.ico`), the manifest and the apple-touch icon; `public/manifest.webmanifest` is hand-written (`theme_color` #050916 = `--zenith`, `background_color` #070a12 = `--background`). The generator takes one source image for every preset, so a separate favicon and maskable source needs either a second config entry / manual export or `<link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml">` to the chosen `favicon.svg`. Installed PWAs cache icons, so the swap shows on a fresh install or after the browser refreshes the manifest.

## Round 2

Owner review (2026-10-17): 2-planet is dropped as the weakest; both stars (1-star and 3-free) have something worth keeping, so round 2 blends them. Four proposals in `evidence/icon-proposals/r2-<n>-<slug>/` (same files as round 1), compared in `comparison-r2.png`; `/design`'s band now shows these four (`design-specimen-r2.png`). Round-1 files stay untouched. In red mode white and gold both become the same red, so there R2-1 matches R2-4 and R2-2 matches round 1's star: colour is only a choice for the normal themes.

- **R2-1 white star, dusk** (`r2-1-white-star-dusk`): round 1's crisp white star (`--primary` #f2f4f8) and `--primary-strong` #c3d0ff companion, set over round 3's sky (`--zenith`, `--horizon`, `--dusk-glow-horizon` #6a3226) and `--background` #070a12 ground. The favicon keeps the companion as a small spark; at 16 px that spark is 2 px and reads as a twinkle, and the ground is a 3 px dark strip.
- **R2-2 gold star, navy** (`r2-2-gold-star-navy`): round 3's gold star (#fde68a, `--star-accent`'s base colour) on round 1's plain `--zenith` → `--horizon` tile, the companion now white (`--primary`). Fewest shapes: the favicon is one gold star on a flat `--zenith` tile, the cleanest of the four at 16 px.
- **R2-3 rising star** (`r2-3-rising-star`): one large white star (round 1) whose lower arm sets behind a low ridge (round 3's horizon, flatter, glow higher up); no companion. The most distinctive silhouette, but at 16 px the cut-off arm makes it read as a short spire over a dark bar rather than an obvious star.
- **R2-4 gold star, dusk** (`r2-4-gold-star-dusk`): round 3 with round 1's companion kept, same layout as R2-1 with the gold main star and the `--primary-strong` companion. Warmest of the four. Small sizes behave as R2-1, with a little less contrast between the star and the glow.

## Final (owner's pick, 2026-10-17)

R2-4's dusk composition (sky gradient, horizon, ground, the main star's position and size) with the main star white (`--primary` #f2f4f8) and no companion, in every variant; the favicon keeps R2-4's 16 px star geometry. Sources and previews in `evidence/icon-proposals/final/` (`final-sheet.png`, `design-specimen.png`); implementation and checks in `plan.md`. The icon is live in `public/` (`favicon.svg`, `favicon.png`, `favicon.ico`, `pwa-icon.svg`, `pwa-maskable.svg` and the generated PNGs), `npm run icons:build` runs one generator config per source, and `/design`'s band now reads the shipped files from `public/` instead of the proposals. Installed PWAs keep their cached icon until a fresh install or the browser refreshes the manifest.
