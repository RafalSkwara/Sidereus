## Notes

M-4 north star (roadmap PR #176, GitHub #172). Galaxy advice in a small telescope must agree with itself: a big bright galaxy such as M31 is not called "very faint" beside "bright for your 60 mm" (#159), and a faint, low-surface-brightness galaxy such as M101 is not ranked high in an 80 mm (#21 item 3). Two rules over the same physics (the expectation's core term and the ranking's surface-brightness penalty), moved together, judged against source-cited references (lessons: "Judge advice that no observer can check against source-cited references").
