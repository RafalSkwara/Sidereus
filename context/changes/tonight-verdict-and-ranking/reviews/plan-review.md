### F1 — Forecast drops the evening hours after UTC midnight

- **Severity**: ❌ CRITICAL
- **Impact**: 🏃 LOW — quick decision; fix is obvious and narrowly scoped
- **Dimension**: Blind Spots
- **Location**: Phase 2 §3
- **Detail**: A live call on 2026-09-25 shows the hourly series starts at 00:00 UTC today. For a night in progress after UTC midnight, the evening hours of the dark window are missing and count as not clear, so the verdict can flip from go to no-go after an hourly refetch.

- **Decision**: FIXED

### F2 — Big objects: the plan differs from the option the user picked

- **Severity**: ⚠️ WARNING
- **Impact**: 🔎 MEDIUM — real tradeoff; pause to reason through it
- **Dimension**: End-State Alignment
- **Location**: Phase 1 §3, Phase 3 §2, brief Open Risks
- **Detail**: The chosen option said the widest eyepiece is shown when nothing fits; the plan returned `none-fit` with no eyepiece to honour the PRD invariant. With a typical kit, M31 and M45 fit nothing.
- **Fix A ⭐ Recommended**: Keep `none-fit` (the invariant holds) but carry the widest eyepiece as advice: "Larger than any field in your kit; use your widest (25 mm) and sweep across it".
  - Strength: Keeps the invariant and still guides the beginner.
  - Tradeoff: Wording close to the edge of the invariant.
  - Confidence: MED — depends on how strictly the invariant is read.
  - Blind spot: None significant.
- **Fix B**: Pair the widest eyepiece with an oversized flag.
  - Strength: Simpler; matches the original option.
  - Tradeoff: Breaks the PRD invariant.
  - Confidence: HIGH.
  - Blind spot: None significant.
- **Decision**: FIXED (Fix A)
